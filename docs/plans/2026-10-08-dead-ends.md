# Dead ends: buttons that say "not in the demo yet"

Status: plan, not started. Build on the user's go.
Branch: `feat/premium-feel`.

A demo viewer who taps one of these gets a snackbar instead of a screen.
This plan wires the ones a viewer is likely to tap, and hides or labels the
rest. Money (Deposit / Withdraw) and sign-in are out of scope here.

## The plan, most-tapped first

### 1–2. Share: one share sheet for people and markets
There's already a share sheet for calls (`share_call_sheet.dart`, the Home
card's Share). Add two cards in the same style:
- **Profile card:** avatar, handle, record ("62 settled, 36 right"), their
  market and cap if they have one, and the last 10 verdicts.
- **Market card:** ticker, price, 7d change with the app's line chart, cap
  and holders.
Same actions as the call share sheet (copy link, save image, share to…),
all simulated.

### 3. More •••: a short action sheet
Follow / Unfollow, Mute their calls, Report, Block, Copy profile link.
Mute and Block are real in the demo: muted or blocked traders drop out of
Home, Arena and Notifications (a `MuteState` set, like `FollowState`).
Report shows a confirmation. This is also the moderation basics the review
flagged.

### 4. Make a call: the existing flow
Make a call on Your market and at the end of Make a market opens the
position picker (`pick_position_screen.dart`) and then the composer, the
same as Arena's +. With no positions, its empty state already points to
Explore.

### 5. Arena search: a results page
When the query isn't an exact ticker or handle, open a results page
instead of the snackbar:
- **Markets:** assets and trader markets matching the name or ticker
  (Leaderboard row cards).
- **People:** traders whose handle matches (row with Follow).
- **Calls:** calls whose text or battle mentions it (call cards).
Tabs: All / Markets / People / Calls. Empty: "Nothing for 'x' yet."

### 6. All callers: the calls list for that market
Opens the market's room on its Top tab (`RoomScreen`), which already lists
every call on that market. No new page.

### 7–8. Images: stay labelled, not tappable
Market image and profile photo need uploads, which the demo doesn't have.
Keep the initial avatar, make the photo circle non-tappable, and keep the
"Photo uploads come later" caption so it reads as planned rather than
broken. Same for Market image: drop the tap, keep a "Later" caption.

### 9. Settings legal and security rows
- **Terms of service / Privacy policy:** a plain in-app text page with the
  version and a placeholder body ("Full text at launch").
- **Delete account:** a confirm sheet ("This removes your profile, calls
  and market. Positions must be closed first.") ending in a simulated
  "Deleted" state that logs out. App stores require the flow to exist.
- **Two-factor:** a toggle with "Set up with an authenticator app", using a
  simulated six-digit code.

## Order and size

1. **Share** (1–2): reuses the call share sheet. Medium.
2. **More •••** (3): action sheet plus `MuteState`. Small to medium.
3. **Make a call** (4): one route each. Small.
4. **All callers** (6): one route. Small.
5. **Arena search results** (5): new page, reuses row and card widgets. Medium.
6. **Settings rows** (9): three small pages and a sheet. Small to medium.
7. **Images** (7–8): copy and tap changes only. Tiny.

Tests for each: the tap leads to the screen or sheet (no snackbar), and
nothing overflows at 360. Mock data only; nothing is sent.
