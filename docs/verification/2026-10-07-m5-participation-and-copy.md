## Verdict

**M5 not exited.** Tests at 5a72ba6 prove that market orders from Home and Arena reach one review flow, and from Maker reach the ticket, which opens review by code; one position and one participation per confirmed action; nothing on cancel, failure or a duplicate confirm; the persona switch, the copied order and one `FeeKind.copyFee` entry per copy the copier persona confirms. Wallet, receipt and ledger totals reconcile arithmetically: the Wallet row and the Your market chip count market credits only (spec 06:10), and spec 06's per-kind footer is unbuilt, so after a copy fee no screen shows why they read less than the Total. Four rows fail: the J4 exit criterion, until the user rules whether "one review flow" covers limit and stop orders, which skip review; the VC-CPY-002 ledger row, which names the copier and asset but not the source call (cosmetic); the VC-CPY-002 creator row, until the user decides what "the creator" means; and the copy story end to end, which rests on that creator row. Three rows are `not run`: the deposit/withdraw toast, the presenter's statement that the fee is invented, and J1–J5 plus the copy story from reset on the Simulator, which the presenter has not run. Each open row of class "misrepresents a financial action" fails the VC-QA-003 bar on its own: limit and stop orders that skip review, the copy-fee payee, the creator persona's copy fee, pre-listing copy fees in a new listing's ledger, the copier's Wallet showing the creator's market, and the five rows carried from the M2–M4 re-verification (maya.eth's market after a fresh listing, other traders' market and Profile pages, a fresh listing's seeded activity, Arena caller prices, and the fixture verdict behind maya.eth's record).
