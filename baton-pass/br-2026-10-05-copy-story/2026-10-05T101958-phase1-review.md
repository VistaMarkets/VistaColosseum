## Design calls
**(a) No copyFee row when the creator copies someone else's call.** Reasonable gap-fill, not literal spec. The spec says every copy appends a row "to the creator ledger" with counterparty = copier handle; read literally, the creator copying kaito.eth would credit herself. The implementer's reason (the author kaito.eth has no ledger in the demo) is sound. But the same reason applies to the copier copying kaito.eth's call, and that case DOES credit maya's ledger (scenario.dart:419). So the rule is inconsistent: maya earns fees on calls she never made, and the ledger heading's own comment ("Flat fees copiers paid to copy the creator's calls", receipt_screens.dart:123) becomes false for those rows. The consistent rule is: credit the creator ledger only when `sourceAuthorHandle == Persona.creator.handle`. That rule makes (a) fall out for free (the own-call rule already stops the creator paying herself). No test pins either behaviour: changing the condition to `copyFee > 0` (credit on every copy) leaves all 296 tests green.

**(b) "The copier cannot copy maya's call in the app." Refuted.** maya.eth's Home feed call (deltaone, trader market) is not tradable, but she also has an asset caller post in `TradeMock.callers` (trade_mock.dart:318-329), shown on every asset trade page. In-app path: Home → ETH card → Details (AssetTradeScreen ETH) → Callers panel → "Everyone" → tap "Open maya.eth's play" → CallerPlayScreen "maya.eth's call" → Long → "Long $200 · 2x" → review shows "Copying @maya.eth · $5.00 copy fee" → Confirm. The existing home_screen_test (≈ lines 1442-1449) already shows maya.eth's post under Everyone at ETH prices. My scratch probe (acting as copier, pushing `CallerPlayScreen.route(mayaPost, 'ETH')`, tapping through the ticket) passed: order filled, ledger row `Copy fee · @sam.sol · ETH 500 maya.eth/ETH`. So the demo story "copier copies the creator, creator earns" works today without a fixture change, and R1's tighter rule would not break it.
