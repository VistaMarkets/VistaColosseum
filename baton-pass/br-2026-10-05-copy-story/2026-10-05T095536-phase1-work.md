## Design calls for the reviewer / manager
1. **Ledger credit only when the copier copies.** Spec says every copy credits the creator ledger with counterparty = copier. When the
   *creator* copies someone else's call she pays the $5 copy fee, but no row lands in her own ledger (it would be a self-credit
   claiming she earned a fee from herself). Copier copies of any author's call credit the creator ledger, per spec.
2. **Behavior change in existing tests:** the creator opening kaito.eth's call on Home is now a copy (spec: "any other call is a copy"),
   so 3 existing tests' figures moved by $5.00: `order_ticket_test` 'feed ticket confirm adds a position…' ($200.20→$205.20, 20020→20520),
   'feed ticket Retry re-quotes…' (same), `home_screen_test` 'the amount slider snaps to its 20% stops' (Max $12,467.53→$12,462.53).
3. maya.eth's only feed call is on a trader market (deltaone), which is not tradable, so in the app the copier cannot copy the
   creator's own call; copier copies of others' asset calls are what credit the ledger. A tradable creator call would need a fixture change (not done).
4. Ledger Total now includes copy fees; Wallet row / Your-market chip still show `marketFeesCents` (market credits only).
