## Ruling on "every record item"
**Carry forward to phase 7; not a phase-6 gap.** The Profile CALLS list and the trader-market Record panel are not lists this spec makes receipt-backed:
1. Behavior defines the backed record: CallReceipts are "seeded from the existing record entries in `market_mock.dart:65-77`. Record lists resolve to **these**." Those 3 entries are maya.eth's own record. `ProfileMock.receipts` and `TraderMarketMock.record` are one generic Figma sample shown under every handle (`profile_mock.dart` doc: "Every profile in the demo shows this content under its own handle"); none of their items is one of "these", and "SOL reaches $300 by Fri" is Right in the seed but Open "2d left" in both samples. Making them resolve means inventing per-trader call fixtures, which is spec 07's stated work ("Derive record metrics from unit-06 CallReceipts per trader", "Seed two fixture traders…", "Seed one trader with zero settled calls").
2. The spec counts and line-cites every other wire ("the three 'All receipts' call sites", "the two 'Call details' row taps (`profile_screen.dart:93`, `trader_market_screen.dart:275`)") and its Gap line lists only the fee constant and All receipts ×3. It cites no record-item call site on profile or trader market.
3. The "unavailable receipt" fallback is granted to Call details only ("or to an 'unavailable' receipt when the fixture names none"); record items "resolve to these", with no fallback.
Carry-forward for phase 7 (CF-1): spec 07's acceptance names no item-tap test, so the obligation can be lost.
