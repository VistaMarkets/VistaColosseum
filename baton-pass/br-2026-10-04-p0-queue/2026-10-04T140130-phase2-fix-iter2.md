### H-1 int64 overflow: FIXED (store, every caller)
- Test: `scenario_test.dart: 'a size the cent math cannot hold fails and changes nothing'` uses the reviewer's values (BTC 1.37e12 units, $67,412, 1x) and a second case at leverage `1 << 50`.
- RED: `Expected: a value greater than <1248000>  Actual: <-9222449699651090332>` (the wrapped `totalCents`).
- Fix in `Scenario.problem` / `OrderIntent`:
  - `_cents` clamps the double to `[0, maxNotionalCents + 1]` before `round()`. `maxNotionalCents = 900719925474` (about $9B, `(2^53−1) ~/ 10000`), so notional × any fee rate stays under 2^53. It is exact as an `int` on VM and web, and no getter can wrap or go negative, even on a refused intent the ticket renders.
  - `problem()` checks `marginCents > cash → notEnoughFunds` before anything sums. Then `notionalCents > maxNotionalCents → 'Size too large'` (only reachable at absurd leverage), then dust, then `totalCents > cash`.
  - Reviewer case → `OrderFailed('Not enough funds')`, `state()` unchanged. Leverage 2^50 case → `'Size too large'`. Tickets show the reason on their disabled button (existing path), so no confirm, toast or negative figure appears.
