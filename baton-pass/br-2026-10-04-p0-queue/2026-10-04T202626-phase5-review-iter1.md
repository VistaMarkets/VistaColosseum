## Findings (severity, file:line, statement)
- **L1** test-strength, `app/lib/features/home/maker_suggestion.dart:79`: No test pins that the card's expiry follows `Scenario.clock`. Mutant `clock-constant` (the card listens to `ValueNotifier(TradeMock.chartEnd)`) survives all 14 maker tests. Behaviour is correct per the probe. Fix: in one widget test, advance `Scenario.clock` past the live expiry and expect "Expired", then `Scenario.reset()` and expect "Trade this".
- **L2** test-strength, `app/lib/features/home/maker_suggestion.dart:111-115`: The reference price and expiry line ("Reference $214.90 · expires in 4h" / "expired 2h ago") is never asserted. Mutant `drop-reference-line` (the line deleted) survives. The spec lists reference price as a fixture field without strictly requiring it on the card, hence LOW.
- **L3** test hygiene, `app/test/home_screen_test.dart:2917`: `find.textContaining('Confirm')` cannot fail here. The feed ticket shows no "Confirm" until its review panel (`order_ticket.dart:956`), so the `BottomSheet` check on the line above is the only real guard (mutation-proven).
- **L4** RED evidence, `app/test/home_screen_test.dart:2834`: The Behavior test `two are seeded on the demo clock, …` has a compile-only RED (`phase-5-red/red-1-compile.log`) and already passed at RED-2. It is real now: reviewer mutants `suggestions-adjacent` and `both-live` kill it. It is not an acceptance item. Every acceptance test has an assertion-level RED in `red-2-assert.log`.
- **L5** maintainability, `app/lib/features/home/home_screen.dart:15` / `:100`: `homeFeed` is a `List<Object>` read with `item as TradeIdea`. A third page kind (for example spec 08's empty or failed states) would crash with a cast error at runtime instead of failing to compile. A sealed feed-item type or an exhaustive `switch` would catch it.
- **L6** pre-existing, a11y, out of phase scope, `app/lib/design_system/components/vista_flow.dart:72`: `VistaPrimaryButton` sets `excludeSemantics: true` and gives its `Semantics` no `onTap`, so even an enabled button exposes no `SemanticsAction.tap`. The probe found the live "Trade this" node with `button=true enabled=true tap=false`, so screen-reader activation of every primary button relies on the platform's fallback behaviour. Not introduced by phase 5. Author call: the fix would be `onTap: enabled ? onPressed : null` on that `Semantics`.

Not filed (manager decision, noted for the user): "Trade this" opens the phase-2 feed ticket priced at the live mark (`MarketPrices.now`), not at the suggestion's reference price. Spec 05 only asks for the asset and direction to be prefilled.
